## 🌟 Our Vision

**Sadaa — an echo of wisdom for the soul from the Qur'an** was born from a deep belief: **the Qur'an speaks directly to the human being, in their lived reality and inner state**.

Depending on one's emotions — calm, doubt, hardship, or hope — certain verses resonate more deeply than others.

Sadaa highlights these resonances by presenting Qur'anic verses **organized by human, spiritual, and existential themes**.

Sadaa is not meant to replace the complete reading of the Qur'an, but to:

- Guide users toward **verses that speak to them here and now**
- Encourage **reflection and contemplation**
- Remind that the Qur'an accompanies the human being **through every stage of life**

> **The Qur'an speaks to you as you are, accompanies your emotions, and guides you on your journey.**

### 📚 Examples of Themes and Categories

The app presents verses organized by:

- **Messages to**: Believers, Disbelievers, Humanity, Children of Israel, People of the Book...
- **Sciences**: Earth Sciences, Cosmology, Biology, Astronomy...
- **Invocations**: Du'as and prayers from the Qur'an
- **Expeditions**: Historical events and Qur'anic narratives
